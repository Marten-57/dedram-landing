DeDram works with U.S. businesses that have older commercial receivables — distributors, manufacturers, commercial service companies, staffing companies, and logistics companies among them.

We typically look at:
  • U.S. B2B receivables
  • 30–180+ days past due

From a first conversation to a completed purchase.

2
