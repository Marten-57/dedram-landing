What we buy

Commercial invoices, not consumer debt.

Typically a fit

  • US business-to-business invoices
  • 30–180+ days past due
  • Debtor is an operating company, not an individual
  • Underlying goods or services were delivered and undisputed
  • Invoice value of $10,000 or more

Not a fit

  • Consumer debt
  • Invoices under active legal dispute
  • Debtors in active bankruptcy proceedings
  • Already-assigned or previously factored invoices
How we work

Underwriting discipline, not a blanket discount rate.

Individually assessed

Every invoice is priced on its own facts — the debtor, the age, and the documentation — not a flat percentage.

Confidential process

Submitting an invoice for an offer does not notify the debtor. Nothing moves until you accept.

Recovery partners

Once purchased, collection is handled through established agency partners operating within applicable federal and state law.
