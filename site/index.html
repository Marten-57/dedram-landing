FAQ

Questions worth answering before you reach out.

Will my customer be contacted?

Not during the review or offer process. If you accept and the receivable is purchased, established third-party recovery professionals take over the follow-up from there — not before.

How do you determine the purchase price?

Each receivable is reviewed individually — the debtor, the age, the documentation, and the likelihood of recovery. There's no fixed formula or blanket discount rate.

What information do I need to provide?

To start, just a high-level description: approximate balance, age, debtor type, and collection history. Detailed account information and supporting documentation are only requested if there's a potential fit, with confidentiality protections established first.

What types of receivables do you purchase?

U.S. business-to-business receivables, typically 30–180+ days past due, where goods or services were delivered and aren't in active dispute or bankruptcy.

What happens after I accept an offer?

We finalize the purchase documentation and terms. Once those are agreed, the receivable is transferred and the agreed purchase price is paid according to those terms.

What if I reject the offer?

Nothing further happens. There's no cost or obligation at any stage before you accept, and declining doesn't affect anything else.
